Knights of Columbus Mass

3-4 times per year

Serve at special Knights of Columbus Masses throughout the year. These Masses highlight the Knights' presence in the parish and provide opportunities to welcome new members.

Ways to Help:

Serve as Eucharistic Minister
Read as Lector
Serve as Minister of Hospitality
Man information table for sign-ups and questions
Issue Yellow Sashes to Knights

Blood Drive

3 times per year

Help organize blood drives with the Indiana Blood Bank and save lives through donations. Each donation can help save up to three lives!

Ways to Help:

Coordinate sign-ups after Mass
Donate blood yourself
Assist donors and provide support
Serve refreshments to donors

Fall Festival - Grillin' Knights

Annual - September

Man the Knights' food booth at St. Luke's annual Fall Festival. Grill burgers and brats, serve hungry festival-goers, and be a visible presence for the Knights in our parish community.

Ways to Help:

Set up booth and equipment
Prep food in the kitchen
Serve food to guests
Grill burgers, brats, and hot dogs
Clean up and take down

Youth Soccer Challenge

Annual - September

Help young athletes test their soccer skills in this fun competition. Kids love showing off their abilities, and you'll help make it a memorable experience!

Ways to Help:

Sign up youth participants
Set up field and equipment
Run the competition
Keep score and encourage kids

40 Days for Life

2 campaigns per year

Join peaceful prayer vigils for the dignity of human life at 86th & Georgetown. Stand as a witness for life and pray for mothers, babies, and an end to abortion.

Ways to Help:

Coordinate sign-ups at Mass
Pray for 1 hour at vigil site
Peaceful witness for life
Invite others to join

Pancake Breakfast

3 times per year

Our famous all-you-can-eat pancake breakfasts raise funds for charitable programs including Coats for Kids and Special Olympics. Great food and fellowship!

Ways to Help:

Set up tables and equipment
Cook pancakes (we'll teach you!)
Serve food to guests
Pour coffee and clean tables
Take down and clean up

Coats for Kids

Annual - Late October

165+ Coats Distributed Annually

Provide warm winter coats to students at Holy Angels School. Make sure every child stays warm during Indiana winters. Seeing the smiles on kids' faces makes this incredibly rewarding!

Ways to Help:

Help coordinate coat purchases
Organize and sort coats by size
Distribute coats at school
Help kids try on and fit coats

Youth Basketball Free Throw Contest

Annual - January

Host the annual youth basketball free throw contest. Help kids test their basketball skills and compete for prizes. Winners can advance to district and state competitions!

Ways to Help:

Advertise the event at parish
Coordinate gym scheduling
Run the competition
Keep score and track results
Encourage young athletes

Baby Bottle Drive

Annual - January/February

Pro-Life fundraiser supporting local pregnancy centers. Partner with the St. Luke Pro-Life Committee to distribute and collect baby bottles filled with donations.

Ways to Help:

Advertise the campaign
Distribute bottles after Masses
Collect filled bottles
Count and deliver donations

40 Cans for Lent

Annual - Throughout Lent

Goal: 10,000 Pounds!

Major food drive benefiting the St. Vincent de Paul Boulevard Place Food Pantry. Help us reach our goal of 10,000 pounds of food to feed families in need.

Ways to Help:

Advertise the food drive
Collect donations at all weekend Masses
Sort and organize collected food
Transport food to Boulevard Pantry

Fish Fry

Two Fridays in Lent

Major Event — Many Volunteers Needed

Our biggest fundraising event! Delicious fried and baked fish dinners served to hundreds of parishioners and community members. Multiple volunteers needed for each dinner to make it a success.

Many Roles Available:

Staff Narthex info tables (all Lent weekends)
Kitchen workers and prep
Door sales and ticketing
Serving line and concessions
Beer & Wine sales
Beer & Wine serving
Beer Baron and Wine Lord coordination
Setup and cleanup

Ways to Help:

Help set up venue
Serve dinner to guests

Clean up after event

Recruit new members by having a Knights presence at the school open house event. Share information about the Knights and invite Catholic men to join.

Ways to Help:

Answer questions about the Knights
Share your Knights experience
Distribute membership information
