Where Your Dues Go

Annual dues cover more than membership — they fund the council's charitable work in the parish and community, cover incidental meeting and event expenses, and pay per-capita fees owed to the State and Supreme Councils that keep us part of the larger Knights of Columbus. Every dollar circles back into either direct charity or the infrastructure that makes our charity possible.

❤

Charitable Works

Parish support, community outreach, and the causes our council backs each year — the reason the Knights exist in the first place.

🏛

Council Operations

Incidental meeting and event expenses, supplies, communications, and the everyday costs of keeping the council running for every member, every event.

🔗

Per-Capita Fees

A portion goes to the State and Supreme Councils — what keeps St. Luke Council connected to the broader Knights of Columbus and its national programs.

The Knights of Columbus was founded to provide for our brothers and their families, and to serve the community around us — dues are how that mission stays funded year over year.

Dues aren't a membership fee in the ordinary sense — they're the mechanism that turns individual membership into collective charity. That's true whether you're able to attend every event or just able to contribute.
