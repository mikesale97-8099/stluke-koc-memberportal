A quick refresher before we begin

Dues aren't a membership fee in the ordinary sense — they're the mechanism that turns individual membership into collective charity. That's true whether you're able to attend every event or just able to contribute in the form of dues and donations.

The vast majority of your dues are used to fund the charitable work we do in our parish and community and to meet the unexpected needs of our brothers and their families. The balance covers the everyday costs of running the council and our per-capita contribution to the State and Supreme Councils that keep us connected to the broader Knights of Columbus family.

We hope you see — and are proud of — the positive impact we make on our parish and fellow knights. Thank you again!
