Thank you for the time and money you give to make our mission possible.

Dues aren't a membership fee in the ordinary sense — they're the mechanism that turns individual membership into collective charity. That's true whether you're able to attend every event or just able to contribute in the form of dues and donations. The vast majority of your dues are used to fund the charitable work we do in our parish and community and the pressing needs that befall our brothers. The balance is used for the everyday costs of running the council and our per-capita contribution to the State and Supreme Councils that keep us connected to the broader Knights of Columbus family. We hope you see and are proud of the positive impact we make on our parish and fellow knights. Thank you again!
